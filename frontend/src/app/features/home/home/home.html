Ethora

A multi-agent deliberation framework where AI agents debate, challenge each other, and reach a verdict through configurable voting mechanisms.

Get Started

How It Works

groups Assemble a Council

Create a council of AI agents, each with a unique role, personality, and area of expertise defined by a custom system prompt.

 forum Deliberate

Agents debate your question across multiple rounds. Each agent sees all prior responses before replying, building a rich discussion in real time.

 how_to_vote Vote & Decide

After deliberation, agents vote using majority, weighted, consensus, or human-in-the-loop mechanisms to reach a final verdict.
